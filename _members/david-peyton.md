David Peyton is an undergraduate student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of New Mexico. His interests include robotics, controls, simulation, FPGA development, PCB design, hardware integration, and aerospace-related research. 

He has worked on projects involving MuJoCo, Isaac Sim, ROS2, small satellite systems and controls, camera and telemetry hardware, and radiation effects testing. He is interested in connecting electrical engineering, robotics, and simulation tools to support real-world research and hardware development.
